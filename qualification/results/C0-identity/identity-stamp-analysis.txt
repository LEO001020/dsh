=== WHY RE-STAMPING THE 314 WOULD BE MISREPORTING ===
  All 314 carry 0a0996f3944b5528..., which IS an entry in the lock's own
  identity_history. It is a REAL past identity of this deployment, not a typo
  and not a placeholder. Those entries are true statements about the artifact
  they measured, at the time they measured it.
  Rewriting the stamp to the current identity without re-running the measurement
  would assert that a measurement was taken against the current artifact when it
  was not. That is exactly the move this project's rules forbid: 'never obtain a
  PASS by ... misreporting a result'. A gate that goes green by re-stamping is
  worse than a gate that stays red, because the red is at least true.

=== WHAT THE GATE ACTUALLY DOES WITH THIS (measured, not assumed) ===
  release-gate.py check 1 records verify-spec's exit code as a CHECK but appends
  NO blocker (lines 230-234). The gate's blocker list is driven by checks 2, 2b,
  3/5/6, 7, 8 and 9. So the 315 verify-spec problems are REPORTED but do not
  themselves make the release NOT_READY -- the FAIL count does.

  This is a deliberate distinction the gate documents: verify-spec 'fails when a
  hash is wrong -- a FILING error, fixable by re-filing evidence, and it says
  nothing about the product'; release-gate 'fails when the candidate is not
  releasable'. One file, two questions, and the tool that answers the easy one is
  not allowed to answer the hard one.

=== THE HONEST POSITION ===
  The 314 identity-stamp mismatches are a STALE-EVIDENCE-INVENTORY signal, and the
  correct remedy is RE-MEASUREMENT under the current identity, case by case -- not
  re-stamping. That is what the round-3 closure did for CMP-02, BR-07, CAP-10 and
  IPY-13, which is why check 9 now reads '3 of 64 assembled-product PASS cases'
  rather than '0 of 61'.
  A future reader should read the 315 as 'this many filed measurements predate the
  current artifact' -- a work list, not a defect list.
