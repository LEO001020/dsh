VER-08 - an acceptance definition cannot authorize itself
==============================================================================

SPEC ORACLE, quoted:
  "The digest check FAILS and the run is refused. Obtaining the authorized digest
   must be a separate step from writing the definition, so a definition cannot go
   green in the same breath it is edited."

THIS IS THE SAME MEASUREMENT AS VER-03 SEEN FROM THE OTHER SIDE, and both are filed
against it deliberately. VER-03 asks whether the candidate's attempt to weaken the
acceptance is DETECTED; VER-08 asks whether the MECHANISM makes self-authorization
impossible in the first place. The load-bearing clause here is the SECOND sentence --
the SEPARATION OF STEPS -- so the capture below shows the two steps as two commands.

STEP 1, obtaining the authorized digest, is a SEPARATE invocation that writes nothing:
  node qualification/runners/acceptance.mjs \
    qualification/results/V9-verification/defs/def-ver02-all-skipped.json --print-digest
  -> the digest is printed to stdout and NOT written back into the definition. The
     runner has no flag that records it. Recording it is a human/reviewed step.

STEP 2, the definition is written with that digest, and STEP 3 re-runs it.
