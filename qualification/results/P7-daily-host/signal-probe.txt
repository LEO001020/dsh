--- what this means for A12 ---
any_catchable_console_event_available_on_win32: true
The ONE catchable route is CTRL_BREAK_EVENT, which Node surfaces as SIGBREAK.
The launcher registers handlers for SIGTERM and SIGINT only, so even that
route does not reach its bounded shutdown: SIGTERM/SIGINT cannot be delivered
catchably here (child.kill and process.kill both terminate without running the
handler), and SIGBREAK has no handler. The Web profile also mounts no
in-product exit command: exitOnStdinEnd is bound by the acp and sdk apps only,
and a piped stdin that is never resumed does not emit end.

Consequence: the host shutdown CANNOT be triggered through any product-
reachable stop route on this platform. The shutdown measurement therefore uses
an instrument route that calls the launcher own ctx.appExit -- the same
callback the product uses -- behind the real trust fence. See
verify-a12-shutdown-route.mjs and FINDINGS.md for how that is labelled.
