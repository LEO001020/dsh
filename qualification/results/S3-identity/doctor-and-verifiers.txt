`--summary` exits 0 and prints the SAME 95/13/1 tally as before the identity move,

The status TALLY is unchanged because this slice changed no verdict. What changed is
the evidence binding: the full run reports 317 problems where it previously reported 3,
and 314 of them read "evidence was filed under identity 0a0996f3944b5528... but the
lock's identity is 533c8cb08b2ccd7f...". A reader who ran only `--summary` would
conclude nothing had changed -- the "a green light appeared once" failure mode. The full
invocation is the one that counts here.
